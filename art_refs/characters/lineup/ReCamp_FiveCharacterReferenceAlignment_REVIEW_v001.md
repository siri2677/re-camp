## Character-specific direction

### Luna

- Keep the strongest first-face read and sensor-cat scout hook.
- Refine eye sparkle, hair grouping and agile feminine posture while preserving the paired-dagger silhouette.
- Do not replace the hook with a generic silver-haired combat girl.

### Miyu

- Keep the asymmetric lavender technician and dual-drone triangle.
- Improve facial appeal and relaxed adult femininity so the sleepy expression does not become childlike or emotionally blank.
- Keep the drones as a character relationship device, not only a weapon attachment.

### Coco

- Keep the warm coral rescue-captain and healthy adult appeal.
- Unify the bright background treatment with the lineup and avoid generic nurse-costume cues.
- Make the cape, injector baton and protection field read as one elegant functional silhouette.

### Iris

- Keep the refined long vertical silhouette and observation-lance identity.
- Increase facial warmth and feminine poise so the design does not collapse into a generic gothic sniper or assassin.
- Preserve the non-firearm observation equipment distinction.

### Noah

- Keep the calm adult guardian and case-to-door shield transformation.
- Keep the face, waist and posture visibly feminine while preventing the dark armor from reading as a gender-ambiguous tank.
- Preserve the shield's shelter function as the visual story hook.
