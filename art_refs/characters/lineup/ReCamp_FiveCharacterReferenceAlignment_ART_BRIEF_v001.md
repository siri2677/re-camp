## Global target

Create attractive, clearly adult female Japanese mobile subculture character art with polished anime faces, expressive eyes, elegant but functional fashion, strong role silhouettes and readable equipment. Use the benchmark qualities from the project direction without imitating any named work's character, costume, weapon, logo, pose, UI or composition.

## Global constraints

- Keep 2D proportion targets at approximately 6.8–7.4 heads and preserve each existing character hook.
- Preserve clean cel-oriented color planes, controlled soft gradients, readable hair masses and adult feminine posture.
- Use one primary appeal axis and two secondary traits per character; do not make all five equally cute, equally exposed or equally glamorous.
- Keep exploration equipment functional and connected to the role; avoid generic nurse, assassin, soldier or tank silhouettes.
- Maintain Re:Camp charcoal, warm gray, cream and character accent language with consistent dark presentation panels.
- Include metadata with reference quality axes, Re:Camp transformation, model/tool, source images, human edits, usage restrictions and originality review.
- Save all corrections as new REVIEW versions. Do not overwrite the current v002 evidence and do not mark APPROVED.

## Character correction briefs

### Luna — bright agile appeal

- Preserve the mint-silver sensor-cat hood, paired energy daggers and fast scout silhouette.
- Push bright eye highlights, a playful but adult smile and a forward-ready asymmetric stance.
- Keep the waist and leg line elegant without turning the design into generic tactical lingerie.
- Use clean cream/charcoal/mint separation and a small pink accent; keep the sensor function readable.

### Miyu — dreamy technical appeal

- Preserve the smoky-lilac asymmetrical bob, one oversized work sleeve and two distinct drones.
- Add a softer but mature expression with visible emotional contrast between neutral and machine-enthusiastic states.
- Keep the drone triangle close to the head/shoulder silhouette and make the relationship with the drones legible.
- Avoid a childlike oversized-head read, school uniform cues or a generic gunner pose.

### Coco — warm healthy appeal

- Preserve coral hair, rescue half-cape, injector baton and jade protection field.
- Replace generic nurse-coded cues with modern rescue-leader fashion, functional harness logic and confident field posture.
- Keep healthy adult curves and a warm face while preserving movement and readable equipment.
- Match the dark lineup presentation background so the KeyArt does not read as a separate visual product.

### Iris — elegant precise appeal

- Preserve deep-plum hair, cold-white asymmetric coat, observation lance and distance ring.
- Add controlled warmth around the eyes and a composed feminine posture without losing precision.
- Make the non-firearm observation function visible through the lens/ring system and stance.
- Avoid generic gothic assassin, school-uniform or conventional rifle-sniper signals.

### Noah — calm protective appeal

- Preserve the midnight-navy bob, protective jacket and case-to-door shield transformation.
- Increase face, waist and posture clarity so the adult feminine silhouette survives the armor and dark palette.
- Keep the shield large enough to communicate shelter but not so dominant that the character disappears.
- Use amber function light and a calm, dependable expression instead of aggressive heavy-tank posing.

## Acceptance checks

- Three-second first impression communicates each face and one-sentence hook.
- 128px faces show distinct eyes, hair and emotional identity.
- Equipment-included and gear-minimal silhouettes identify all five.
- Low-saturation and color-vision checks retain role and character separation.
- All five read as adult women without relying on identical exposure or sexualized posing.
- Presentation background, lighting and finish are consistent across the lineup.
- No external work's traceable character or design element is copied.
- Human reviewer, date and decision remain pending until the user evaluates the package.
