## Palette lock

- `Technical Black`: matte cloth, hair shadow masses, shorts, boots, and primary value anchor.
- `Forest Green`: coat lining and large utility-panel fields.
- `Teal Accent`: inner/back hair and limited equipment insert; controlled small-area accent.
- `Copper Gold`: restrained hardware, ring frame, buckles, chains, and gauntlet edge parts.
- `Natural Pale Skin`: soft satin response without extreme tinting.
- `Warm Amber`: eye accent only; small identity signal.
- `Gauntlet Black`: matte articulated plates with controlled copper edge separation.

## Material direction

- Hair: grouped non-metallic black and teal masses with controlled highlights.
- Skin: soft satin response.
- Black and forest-green outfit: mostly matte technical fabric with limited trim sheen.
- Hardware and anchor ring: restrained low-to-medium metal response; small-area accent only.
- Gauntlets: matte black segmented plates, copper-gold edge hardware, and a limited teal insert.
- Activation: maintain the source palette; no full-screen glow or new neon family.
