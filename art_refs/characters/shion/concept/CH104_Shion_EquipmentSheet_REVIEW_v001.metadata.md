## Equipment package

### Folding prism fan

- `A Neutral / Stored`: compact folded compass-like form with a short indigo grip.
- `B Ready`: half-open faceted fan with the same central pivot and grip.
- `C Full Survey`: fully opened geometric fan with a narrow straight surveying light direction.
- Keep the object reading as a surveying instrument, not a staff, bow, shield, or firearm.

### Single map ring

- One oval ring frame with one suspended faceted map core.
- The same object may be lowered/quiet or activated near the hand; do not create independent orbit-ring clones.
- The front view, side view, and hand/hip inset are scale and carry evidence, not a second equipment choice.
