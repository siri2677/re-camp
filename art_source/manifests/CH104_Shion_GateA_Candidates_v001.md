## 1. Morning roster visual lock

- Identity source: the fourth figure from the left in `CH101-105_Roster_FaceDiversity_WIP_v003.png`.
- Face: narrow indigo eyes, straight brows, long angular face, pointed jaw, restrained closed mouth.
- Hair: deep indigo long layers, hot-magenta tips, long asymmetrical bangs.
- Outfit: porcelain-ivory short jacket dress, deep indigo high-waist geometric panels, smoke-gray accents, hot-magenta edge/light accents.
- Equipment: one folding prism fan and one single map ring.
- Silhouette: tall, straight, long-legged, and geometric; no wide curved medic veil silhouette.
